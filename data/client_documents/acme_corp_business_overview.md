# Acme Corp — Business Overview

Acme Corp operates several enterprise business units and has been investing in process automation.

The client initially adopted the Workflow Automation Platform to reduce repetitive operational tasks. Early adoption was positive, but the client later reported that some integrations required more engineering effort than expected.

The operations team is particularly interested in:
- Faster reporting
- Better workflow monitoring
- Reduced manual intervention
- Reliable integrations
- Clear ROI reporting

The engineering team has requested better API documentation and clearer escalation paths for integration issues.

The executive team is evaluating whether to expand the platform to additional business units during the next planning cycle.
